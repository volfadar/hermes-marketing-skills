# Platform fit — choose from buyer behaviour and operator capacity

Platform norms change, and generic “best time”, hashtag counts, engagement
benchmarks, or posting cadences transfer badly between accounts. There is **no
universal best time or cadence**. Treat any current platform limit as a fact to
verify from official documentation; treat performance advice as a test against
the owner's own data.

## Pick the buyer channel first

Ask where the last real buyers discovered, asked, compared, ordered, and paid.
The answer may be a physical location, WhatsApp, a marketplace, Instagram,
TikTok, search/Maps, LinkedIn, a community, or a referral partner. Do not infer
the buyer channel from nationality, industry, age, or revenue alone.

Start with one channel when time is tight. Add another only when it serves a
different job and the operator can maintain it.

## Fit the format to available proof

| Material the owner has | Useful shapes to test |
|---|---|
| product photo | availability, comparison, use occasion, catalogue answer |
| customer chat with permission/anonymisation | FAQ, objection, customer wording |
| voice note | short text, narration, FAQ answer |
| screen/work sample | process, before/after with evidence, walkthrough |
| no content archive | short owner interview, buyer questions, product facts |

Reels, carousels, threads, long video, live sessions, and trends are options,
not obligations. A format outside `ACCESS`, inside `REFUSE`, or beyond the time
`CAP` is a failed recommendation.

## Cadence and timing

Use this order:

1. what the owner actually published last period;
2. the capacity they commit for this test;
3. the account's own **account insights** for active audience times;
4. a small labelled experiment when the account has no history.

Do not fill a calendar from an industry cadence table. Empty/rest days are
valid. Do not claim a time is “best” unless the account data shows it. The
number of slots is a cap supplied by the owner, not a target generated by the
tool.

## Match metrics to the business job

Choose the smallest useful chain:

```text
content seen in the right context
→ qualified enquiry or useful reply
→ quote/menu/catalogue opened
→ order/deposit/booking
→ contribution or repeat purchase
```

Reach, likes, saves, watch time, and comments may diagnose a creative step.
They are not commercial success by themselves. Record orders and gross profit
or contribution where the owner can do so safely.

## Platform mechanics

Use official, current sources only when exact limits matter: supported media,
caption limits, API availability, scheduling capability, account requirements,
and advertising/disclosure rules. Open the source in the current session. Do
not import a global report as the local account's expected result.

## Anti-patterns

- identical cross-posting without adapting context;
- buying followers/engagement or using engagement pods;
- automated unsolicited DMs;
- invented customer quotes, results, “best times”, or category benchmarks;
- choosing several channels before one produces a useful business signal;
- forcing a creator format the owner will not sustain.
