# Big Five × O*NET RIASEC

## What each input can and cannot answer

| Input | Useful for | Cannot prove |
|---|---|---|
| O*NET RIASEC | preferred activity patterns and environments | competence, market demand, income, channel fit |
| Big Five | possible work-style and energy accommodations | talent, destiny, business success |
| CV/portfolio | experience, proof, repeated exposure, access | current demand or willingness to buy |
| Market research | customer language, alternatives, context, constraints | the user's ability to execute |
| Experiments | observed response in a defined context | universal long-term success |

Build strategy from the intersection, not from a two-test matrix.

## RIASEC as interview prompts

| Code | Activity interest | Useful follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| R — Realistic | making, operating, repairing, physical systems | What have you built or operated repeatedly? |
| I — Investigative | analyzing, diagnosing, researching | Which problems do people ask you to investigate? |
| A — Artistic | creating, composing, interpreting | Which work samples show a distinct judgment or craft? |
| S — Social | teaching, helping, facilitating | Which transformation have you helped another person achieve? |
| E — Enterprising | persuading, leading, initiating | What have you sold, organized, negotiated, or mobilized? |
| C — Conventional | structuring data, process, detail | Which error, delay, or cost have your systems reduced? |

Ask one question at a time. The highest code is not automatically the business category.

## Cross-reference method

1. Identify the top RIASEC activities and ask for concrete examples.
2. Use Big Five to test operating conditions that may sustain those activities.
3. Inspect CV/portfolio for proof, repeated exposure, and buyer access.
4. Research whether a buyer has a meaningful unresolved job.
5. Design an experiment that the user's working style can sustain.

Example:

```text
RIASEC C/E
  → hypothesis: enjoys structured commercial operations
CV: handled product imports, variants, and returns
  → proof: knows marketplace failure points
Research: seller complaints and service alternatives
  → market question: which failure is costly enough to pay to remove?
Experiment
  → sell a scoped diagnostic or operational fix to a small sample
```

The strategy emerges from the chain. “C/E means Shopee admin” would be an unsupported shortcut.

## Contrasts

A contrast can create an operating advantage after validation:

- analytical + socially comfortable may help translate complex evidence in live workshops;
- creative + highly structured may support a repeatable design system;
- service-oriented + assertive may help facilitate difficult decisions;
- practical + curious may improve existing products without chasing novelty.

Do not advertise the contrast itself. Convert it into a buyer-visible mechanism, proof, or service.

## Failure modes

- recommending TikTok, LinkedIn, or WA from a score table;
- treating Social as “must coach” or Artistic as “must create content”;
- using a contrast as a brand claim without customer relevance;
- ignoring work the user can prove because it does not match their top code;
- rejecting a viable channel the buyer uses because the user's Extraversion is low;
- confusing interest with skill or willingness to perform the commercial parts of a role.
