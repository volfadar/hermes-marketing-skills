# Philosophy — Evidence Before Scale

The skill exists to prevent premature execution. Its order is:

```text
decision → working hypotheses → dossier → live research → positioning choices
         → smallest experiment → tools for that experiment → journey graph
```

This is not a rigid workshop script. A user may arrive with a short question,
partial test result, old CV, unfinished portfolio, existing product, or live
business. Hermes gathers what is missing over ordinary turns and moves backward
when new evidence changes the decision.

## Tests are hypotheses

Big Five and O*NET can suggest questions about working conditions, interests,
and tolerable tasks. They cannot decide a channel, profession, niche, or business
model on their own. Confirm test interpretations with observed behavior and
record accommodations rather than personality prescriptions.

## Artifacts turn familiarity into proof

A CV supplies chronology and claims. A portfolio, work sample, metric, repeated
task, or trusted relationship supplies stronger proof. Separate the two. Also
record dislikes, failures, inaccessible markets, cash/time limits, and missing
evidence; these often change the recommendation more than a strength label.

## Research can reject the attractive story

Do not research only to decorate an idea with citations. Check customer
language, direct and substitute alternatives, buying context, channel rules,
and economics. A useful research pass can narrow, reshape, or kill the initial
position. Mark facts, inferences, and experiments distinctly.

## Positioning must change operations

A useful position gives the market a remembered cue and a credible promise. It
also determines what the business must do differently: product form, guarantee,
packaging, proof, distribution, service procedure, price, or exclusion. “For a
smaller audience” is not enough when the offer remains indistinguishable.

## Learn before installing a stack

Choose tools for a named experiment or operating job. A manual spreadsheet and
five consented conversations may be the correct stack for week one. Content,
broadcast, and research automation become useful only when the route, permission,
and economics justify them.

## A journey is a graph, not a mandatory ladder

Different segments may need different entries and bridges. A high-friction
offer may use diagnosis, sampling, an event, a small paid product, partnership,
or affiliate route before the core offer. A low-margin purchase may need a much
shorter path. Design from budget, contribution margin, capacity, repeat behavior,
and buying friction—not from a universal content → lead magnet → webinar → sale
template.

## Human marketing has concrete safeguards

Use consented contacts, truthful disclosure, frequency limits, and human review
where context matters. “Soft” wording does not make unsolicited outreach ethical
or effective. Measure complaints, opt-outs, acquisition cost, completion, repeat,
and contribution—not follower counts alone.

## Relationship to operational skills

- `cloakserve-research` or another browser gathers evidence before the decision.
- `content-creator` produces a specific test asset or proven content system.
- `waha-marketing` serves permissioned communication with opt-out controls.

Brand strategy coordinates these jobs. It is not automatically “first and then
finished”; research and customer evidence continue to revise the strategy.
