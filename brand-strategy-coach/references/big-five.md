# Big Five in Strategy Coaching

## Use it as a hypothesis generator

The Big Five describes broad personality tendencies. It does not establish talent, business potential, mental health, channel performance, or occupational fit.

Use a score to ask a better behavioral question or design a sustainable operating accommodation. Confirm it against work examples, portfolio behavior, and the user's own correction.

## Domains

| Domain | Higher score may indicate | Lower score may indicate | Strategy question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Openness | curiosity, novelty, abstraction | practicality, familiarity, convention | Does the user enjoy inventing frames or improving a proven format? |
| Conscientiousness | structure, persistence, planning | flexibility, spontaneity, variable routine | Which system helps the user ship repeatedly? |
| Extraversion | social stimulation, assertiveness, visible energy | lower social stimulation, reflection, selective interaction | Which selling activities energize or drain them? |
| Agreeableness | cooperation, empathy, harmony | skepticism, competition, direct challenge | How do they handle negotiation, boundaries, and disagreement? |
| Negative Emotionality/Neuroticism | sensitivity to threat/stress | emotional stability under uncertainty | Which risk controls make experimentation sustainable? |

Neither pole is inherently better. A lower score is not a defect and a higher score is not proof of skill.

## Facets

Facets can explain why a domain-level generalization misses. Use the facet names supplied by the actual test because instruments differ.

Examples:

- high Extraversion with low sociability but high assertiveness may fit a prepared demo better than constant networking;
- high Openness with low aesthetic interest may appear as intellectual experimentation, not visual creativity;
- high Conscientiousness with low organization but high industriousness may need a simple queue rather than a complex planning system;
- high Agreeableness with strong honesty may support trusted advising but require explicit price boundaries;
- high Negative Emotionality with low volatility may mean worry before launch but steady behavior during problems.

Do not over-interpret one extreme facet without a behavioral example.

## Three-step interpretation

1. **Describe:** State one or two plain-language hypotheses relevant to the decision.
2. **Validate:** Ask for a recent example where the tendency did or did not appear.
3. **Accommodate:** Adjust cadence, role, channel format, or risk control.

Example:

> Hipotesisnya, percakapan sosial berulang mungkin lebih menguras tenaga daripada presentasi yang terstruktur. Dalam pekerjaan terakhir, mana yang lebih berat: membuka obrolan, memimpin demo, atau follow-up?

If the user's example contradicts the score, trust the observed context for the current decision.

## Channel evaluation

Never select a channel from personality alone. Evaluate:

```text
buyer behavior
× existing audience/access
× evidence format required
× unit economics
× user skill and willingness to practice
× sustainable energy/cadence
```

Personality may change how the channel is operated:

| Observed need | Possible accommodation to test |
|---|---|
| social energy drops quickly | fewer qualified calls, scripts, asynchronous proof |
| novelty causes unfinished work | one experiment queue, fixed shipping cadence |
| routine causes boredom | stable format with rotating research topics |
| conflict avoidance hurts pricing | scoped packages, written boundaries, review rule |
| launch anxiety delays action | pre-registered small test, budget cap, partner check-in |
| impulsive public commitments | 24-hour review for expensive/irreversible decisions |

These are options to validate, not score-based prescriptions.

## O*NET relationship

Combine Big Five only after reading the O*NET result:

- RIASEC suggests activities that attract the user.
- Big Five suggests possible working-style accommodations.
- CV/portfolio shows competence and proof.
- Research shows demand and buying behavior.
- Economics and constraints decide feasibility.

No pair of tests can replace the other three layers.

## Do not do

- infer a niche from the top two domains;
- diagnose anxiety, depression, trauma, or another condition;
- call Neuroticism a weakness or risk score;
- claim a user cannot sell, lead, create video, or build community;
- convert MBTI, CliftonStrengths, or another instrument into precise OCEAN values;
- repeat scores to simulate personalization;
- recommend a public channel before buyer and market evidence exist.
